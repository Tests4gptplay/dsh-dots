# DSH Dots

Use [ChatGPT Dots](https://chatgpt.com/dots/home) directly inside DeepSeek Harness. Desktop preloads Dots at startup, keeps the webpage and its state when switching DSH panels, and offers Reload and Open in browser controls.

The toolbar and ring icon adapt to DSH's light and dark themes, with Chinese and English interface text. Sign in to ChatGPT on the official website.

**Login retention (Desktop):** Open **DSH Settings → Dots Login Retention** to check status, apply, or restore the Dots-specific persistent browser setup.
